# Frame: first playtest

The author played the Phase 2 build on his phone (2026-10-24).

## What he said, and what each point is

| The author | Class | Response |
|---|---|---|
| "The money aspect is tough in the first province, there is no income until you take it... the upkeep hits very hard without more than 1 province." | Design | Seize the provincial treasury when the province falls; legions in their home province live partly off local requisitions (reduced pay); a legate's allowance in Act I |
| "A lot of what happens is confusing because you don't see enough explanation." | Missing explanation | Monthly report; "What now?" objective card; short guides (battle, provinces) |
| "My army got slaughtered... I didn't see what my troop size was... There should be a retreat option." | Missing control | Pre-battle card with both sides' strength and Withdraw; Sound the retreat during a battle |
| "My legion control tab was confusing... it would be nice if I could control multiple armies, like if I get a general to swear to me." | Missing control and design | Army screen rebuilt around armies and garrisons; generals who command armies of their own |
| "I didn't see where the building tab was." | Missing explanation | Prominent tabs; Build on the overview and the provinces list |
| "It was very easy to promise... then almost never get a chance to pay it back." | Design | Due date and income shown when promising; pay part to buy time |
| "Every month should have a overview page... not something that takes 5 minutes to read." | Missing explanation | One-screen month report |
| "It definitely needs a bit more story in it." | Design | Opening pages, act pages, character events with choices |
| "I typed in my characters name and you can't see the text." | Bug | Fixed: dark-mode phones painted white text into a white box |

## Status
All nine points answered in the build of 2026-10-26 (`docs/DESIGN.md` §15).
