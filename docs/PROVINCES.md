# Provinces and legions, AD 68–69

Draft reference for the map and the starting order of battle. **Status: draft
from standard sources, to be checked line by line before it becomes game
data.** Entries marked "(verify)" are ones where sources disagree or the
arrangement changed during 68–69.

Counting rules used here:
- The two Germanies are counted as provinces. Formally they were military
  districts of Gallia Belgica until about AD 85, but each had its own consular
  legate and legions, which is what matters for the game.
- Procurator-run districts (the Alpine provinces, Raetia, Noricum, Thracia,
  the Mauretanias, Cappadocia) are counted.
- Client kingdoms (Commagene, Emesa, Agrippa II's kingdom, the Bosporus) are
  not provinces; they appear on the map as allied powers.
- Italy is not a province. It is split into four game regions.

Total: **37 provinces + 4 Italy regions.**

## Legion count

About **28–32 legions** across 68–69: the 28 standing in 66, plus I Italica
(Nero, 66–67), I Adiutrix (from the Misenum fleet, 68), VII Galbiana (Galba, 68)
and I Macriana (Macer, 68, disbanded after his death). Several Rhine legions
were disbanded after the Batavian revolt (70), outside the main game window.
The count at game start (March 68) should be fixed from the verified table.

Legions in motion during 68–69, which matter for the opening:
- **XIV Gemina** — withdrawn from Britain c. 66 for Nero's planned eastern
  campaign; in Dalmatia or Italy in 68–69; fought for Otho.
- **I Italica** — raised for the same campaign; at Lugdunum in 69.
- **X Gemina** — Pannonia to Spain, 68.
- **III Gallica** — Syria to Moesia, late 68.

## Grain

Josephus (*Jewish War* 2.383–386, in Agrippa's speech) has Africa feeding Rome
for eight months of the year and Egypt for four. Treat as approximate. It sets
the game's grain shares: whoever holds Africa and Egypt can starve Rome.
