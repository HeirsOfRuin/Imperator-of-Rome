Imperator

A turn-based strategy game of the Roman civil wars, AD 68-71.

You are a legionary legate the history books never mention. Nero is falling, and four men will hold the throne in a single year. Win your province, take the legions, and march on Rome against Galba, Otho, Vitellius and Vespasian. Every month you choose where your money, your men and your name go.

The game

  • Monthly turns across the whole empire: govern provinces, raise and pay legions, move armies, read the month's dispatches.
  • Every encounter is a choice: buy them, negotiate, fight, or march past. Battles are fought on a pixel field with orders to your cohorts.
  • The Senate, the frontier peoples and the other claimants remember what you did. Bought legions waver; broken truces cost you legitimacy.
  • One win: take Rome, be recognised by the Senate, and hold it (13, 18 or 23 months by difficulty). You lose if you fall, retire, or run out of time.

About it

  • Plays in the browser, on a phone or a computer. Installs from the web version and plays offline.
  • Saves in your browser. The menu's save code moves a game between devices.
  • Free. No account, no ads, no analytics. Its typefaces ship inside the game, so nothing leaves your device.

Made with AI assistance. I designed the game and its systems and directed, edited and play-tested it; the code, the text and the art were generated with an AI model to that design.
